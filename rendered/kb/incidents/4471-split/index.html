parser · the shared parser reads dates month first

Tenants who write the day first get their dates back with day and month swapped: “Our 7 April reminder went out on 4 July.” It reaches every day-first tenant, through the app, email and the booking-v2 form, whose hint already asks for the day first.

Fixed. fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales reads dates in the tenant’s order; #4471 closed on 5 October. Ticket closed as completed.

hint · booking-v3 tells day-first tenants to type the month first

booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type what the hint tells them, and the form reads it the tenant’s way, day first: the same swap, from a different bug. Only form reports from tenants on booking-v3 have it. A parser fix does not reach it.

Won’t fix. The booking-v3 form is retired, and its hint with it. Ticket closed as not planned.

one-day · dates move by one day

These reports show no swap. “Invite says Tuesday 12 May. Calendar says Monday 11 May.” “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday. Same meeting.” Swapping day and month cannot move a date by one day; turning a date into a time in one zone and back in another can. The desk noted them in desk-notes.json (oneDayMove) and files them under parser by tenant.

Open. The parser fix does not change a date by one day, and none of these came through a booking form. Next: find where a booking’s date passes through a time zone, and keep it a calendar date.
